# Engineering Mastery Roadmap

This roadmap is an evolving guide, not a promise that every topic is already available. Check items off only when the lesson content and practice material are present.

## How to use this roadmap

- Follow the suggested order, or jump to a topic when you have the prerequisites.
- Read the lesson, run the examples, attempt the exercises, and review the solutions afterward.
- Keep a short note of what was confusing and revisit it later.
- Contributions that improve clarity and correctness are welcome.
