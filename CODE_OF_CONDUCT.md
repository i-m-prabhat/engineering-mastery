# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place to learn and collaborate, especially for people who are new to software engineering or open source.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.
- Make room for different backgrounds, experience levels, and ways of learning.
- Accept feedback gracefully and help keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, and unwanted sexual attention are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private information without permission, or disrupt discussions through abusive or deliberately hostile behavior.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness behavior that does not meet these expectations, contact the project maintainer privately through GitHub rather than escalating the discussion publicly. Please include enough context to help us understand what happened.

The maintainers will review reports fairly and take any action they consider appropriate, including a warning, removal of content, or restricting participation. They will respect the privacy of people who report concerns to the extent possible.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and other community discussions.
